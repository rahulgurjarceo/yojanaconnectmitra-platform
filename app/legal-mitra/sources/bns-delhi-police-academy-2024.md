# BNS Legal Source

Source: Delhi Police Academy, Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) Booklet, Version 2, May 2024.

Use this source as a reference only. Legal Mitra must show the source and page reference and should verify current law against authoritative current sources before presenting legal information as current.

Sections in the booklet include BNS overview, IPC-to-BNS mapping, newly added/partially added provisions, removed IPC provisions, selected BNSS provisions, punishment-duration indexes, and commonly used policing sections.

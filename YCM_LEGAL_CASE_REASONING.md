# Legal Mitra Case Reasoning Engine

The reasoning layer is designed as decision support for qualified lawyers.

## Output layers

1. Fact separation: proven / alleged / disputed / unknown.
2. Issue map: legal elements and procedural questions.
3. Evidence map: what exists, what is missing and what should be preserved.
4. Behavioural/psychology lens: neutral interviewing, cognitive-bias checks, conflict dynamics and communication patterns.
5. Alternative scenarios: competing factual explanations instead of a single predicted outcome.
6. Opposing-side analysis: strongest plausible counterargument and what evidence would answer it.
7. Lawyer questions: targeted questions before strategy is finalized.
8. Risk flags: deadlines, jurisdiction, evidence, safety and high-stakes review.
9. Source verification: legal claims must be tied to primary/current authority before external use.

## Safety boundary

The engine must not diagnose a client/witness/opponent, infer guilt from demeanour, predict a judge's decision, provide a win probability, or replace qualified legal judgment. It is not a substitute for professional legal advice or representation.
